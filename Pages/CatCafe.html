Kitt'n Coffee

5 Month-long, published cat café idle game

The project
Kitt'n Coffee is an idle game in which the player manages his own cat café. The player can discover up to 15 cats, 5 recipes as well as customizing his café in a lot of different ways !
My main roles during this project were :

  • Balancing : Developing balancing tools and workflows and applying them to try and make the user experience more fun and engaging, while maintaining a sufficient playtime and a rewarding progression
  • Producing : Setting deadlines, making sure they are respected and assigning tasks
  • Game Design : Creating and iterating on the base concept and mechanics, depending on the target audience and several rounds of feedback
  • QA Testing : Organizing targeted playtest sessions and formalizing the feedback, then coordinating the programmers and artists to fix reported issues
