Kitt'n Coffee

5 Month-long, published cat café idle game

The project
Kitt'n Coffee is an idle game in which the player manages his own cat café. The player can discover up to 15 cats, 5 recipes as well as customizing the furniture of his café !
My main roles during this project were :

  • Balancing : Developing balancing tools and workflows and applying them to try and make the user experience more fun and engaging, while maintaining a sufficient playtime and a rewarding progression
  • Producing : Setting deadlines, making sure they are respected and assigning tasks
  • Game Design : Creating and iterating on the base concept and mechanics, depending on the target audience and several rounds of feedback

My work
In Balancing :

  • Coordinating with Programmers to develop the tools I needed to set up a clean Balancing workflow
  • Setting up and maintaining Google Sheets tools, helping for Data Analysis
  • Preparing, integrating and testing values through targeted playtest sections
  • Documenting the system and explaining what were the main mathematical challenges



In Producing :

  • Communicating with Game Designers, Game Artists and Programmers to set up an efficient pipeline
  • Adapting the scope depending on the volume of work every student could output
  • Preparing and managing weekly meetings and their reports
  • Organizing our planning around biweekly playtest sections





In Game Design :

  • Creating the concept with Jules BROSSARD, from a pitch of Blanca LAINEZ
  • Writing and maintaining the documentation up to date depending on the changes made to the mechanics
  • Communicating with Artists and Programmers on how to make cats cuter


Conclusion
Kitt'n Coffee has been a challenging project but to this day, it still is one of the projects I'm most proud of.

This project allowed me to work on several skills I wanted to develop, both hard-skills and soft-skills. For example, I was able to work freely on Balancing, as well as put my hands on some real player data, for Data Analysis.

For soft-skills, I learned a lot about communicating with other fields, each with their own languages and needs. I also could work on being even more rigorous and disciplined on my own work.
