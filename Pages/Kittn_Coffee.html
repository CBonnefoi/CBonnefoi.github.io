Kitt'n Coffee

5 Month-long, published cat café idle game

The project
Kitt'n Coffee is an idle game in which the player manages his own cat café. The player can discover up to 15 cats, 5 recipes and customize his furniture !
My main roles during this project were :

  • Balancing : Developing balancing tools and workflows and applying them to try and make the user experience more fun and engaging, while maintaining a sufficient playtime and a rewarding progression
  • Producing : Setting deadlines, making sure they are respected and assigning tasks
  • Game Design : Creating and iterating on the base concept and mechanics, depending on the target audience and several rounds of feedback

My work
In Balancing :

  • Coordinating with Programmers to develop the tools I needed to set up a clean Balancing workflow
  • Setting up and maintaining Google Sheets tools, helping for Data Analysis
  • Preparing, integrating and testing values through targeted playtest sections
  • Documenting the system and explaining what were the main mathematical challenges
To balance Kitt'n Coffee, we used .csv files at first, helping us to get a total control over any value in the game. Those files would typically look like these :


We ended up changing the way we worked towards the end, because we wanted to allow players to play for a long time, meaning formulas became easier to manage in the limited time we had : having a single .csv file for over 30 furnitures, each with 12 levels became too long to fine-tune, compared to simple formulas.

The most challenging part of balancing Kitt'n Coffee was trying to get the rythm right. Just like any Idle Game, we had to get the rythm right to make the game satisfying. In order to not lose another 40+ minutes every time we made a simple change, I made several tools on Google Sheets allowing us to better understand what every change would do.


We also set up a logging tool, allowing us to have a deeper look at what the players were really doing. This tool also helped us track a lot of bugs we wouldn't have been able to detect otherwise.

  • Adapting the scope depending on the volume of work every student could output

In Game Design :

Conclusion
Kitt'n Coffee has been a challenging project but to this day, it still is one of the projects I'm most proud of.

This project allowed me to work on several hard-skills and soft-skills I wanted to develop. For example, I was able to work freely on Balancing, and put my hands on some real player data, for Data Analysis.

I also learned a lot about communicating with other fields, each with their own languages and needs. I also focused on being even more rigorous and disciplined on my work.
